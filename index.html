Forensic Economic Loss Calculator

Present-value estimation of lost earning capacity for personal injury, wrongful death, and wrongful termination matters

For preliminary estimation only. This tool applies standard forensic economics conventions (wage growth, fringe benefits, worklife expectancy, personal consumption offset, and present-value discounting). It is not a substitute for a full analysis by a qualified forensic economist, does not incorporate jurisdiction-specific worklife/mortality tables (e.g. Skoog-Ciecka, BLS), and should not be relied upon as the sole basis for a report, settlement, or testimony.

Methodology & Formulas
  • Loss period: from Date of Loss to Retirement Age (from Date of Birth) or a fixed Worklife Years figure, whichever method is selected.
  • Annual base: Year i gross earnings = Base Earnings × (1 + growth rate)i; total compensation = gross earnings × (1 + fringe rate).
  • Personal Injury deduction: residual post-injury earning capacity (grown at the same wage growth rate) is subtracted from total compensation.
  • Wrongful Death deduction: total compensation is reduced by the personal consumption rate to isolate the portion that would have accrued to survivors.
  • Past loss: the portion of each annual period occurring before the Evaluation Date is summed at nominal value, optionally compounded forward to the Evaluation Date at the prejudgment interest rate.
  • Future loss: the portion of each annual period occurring after the Evaluation Date is discounted back to the Evaluation Date using the mid-period convention: PV = Amount ÷ (1 + discount rate)t, where t is the time from the Evaluation Date to the midpoint of that period.
  • Total Economic Loss = Past Loss (with interest, if any) + Present Value of Future Loss.
